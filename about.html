About the project

Making energy information easier to understand

This student website demonstrates a multi-page structure, consistent CSS styling and interactive JavaScript features.

Our purpose

A simple guide for Australian households

EnergyWise introduces the relationship between appliance power, operating time and electricity cost. It is designed as an educational demonstration rather than a source of official energy advice.

The website focuses on clear navigation, readable content and simple tools that respond directly to user input.

Project features

  • Three connected HTML pages
  • Shared external stylesheet
  • JavaScript FAQ accordion
  • Interactive energy calculator
  • Responsive and accessible layout
